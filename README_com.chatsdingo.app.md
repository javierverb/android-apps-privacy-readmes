# [Privacy Policy for ChatsDingo](#privacy-policy-for-chatsdingo)

Effective Date: May 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction

ChatsDingo ("the App", "we", "us") is a mobile IRC chat client for Android (`com.chatsdingo.app`). The App connects your device directly to third-party IRC networks that you choose (for example IRC-Hispano, Libera.Chat, or OFTC). We do not operate those IRC networks and we do not run a proprietary chat backend for message routing.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide in the App

- **IRC nickname**: Required to join a network. You may connect as a guest nick or use a registered nick.
- **NickServ registration data** (optional): If you register a nick through the App, your chosen password and email address are sent to the IRC network's NickServ service via standard IRC commands. We do not receive or store this data on our servers.
- **Messages you send**: Chat text is transmitted to the IRC network and channel or user you select, under that network's rules.

### 2.2 Information stored on your device

The App stores some data locally only (not on servers we control):

- **Blocked contacts**: IRC nicknames you block are saved on your device using local app storage.
- **IRC network preference**: Your selected IRC network preset (when applicable).
- **Channel list cache**: A temporary cache of public channel listings to improve browsing performance.

Chat history while connected exists in app memory during your session. We do not maintain a central archive of your conversations.

### 2.3 Information collected by third parties

**IRC networks (third-party operators)**  
When you connect, the IRC server and its operators may log connection metadata (such as IP address, nick, channels joined) and message content according to their own policies. Communications use the IRC protocol; default network presets in the App may use unencrypted TCP (port 6667). Messages are **not** end-to-end encrypted by ChatsDingo.

**Google AdMob (advertising)**  
The App integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google may collect and process data such as:

- Device identifiers (including Android Advertising ID where available)
- IP address and device information
- App interactions and ad engagement
- Diagnostic and performance data related to ads

This data is handled under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Ads policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use locally stored preferences only to operate App features (blocking, network selection, list caching). We do not use your IRC messages for profiling or marketing on our own systems because we do not host those messages.

AdMob is used to fund the free App and may use collected data to serve and measure ads.

## 4. Permissions

The App requests:

- **INTERNET**: Required to connect to IRC networks and to load advertisements.

Additional permissions may be merged from the Google Mobile Ads SDK (for example advertising ID access) as required by Google Play services.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not operate backend servers that receive your IRC chat content. Data is shared only as follows:

- **With IRC networks** you connect to, when you send commands or messages.
- **With Google** through the AdMob SDK, as described above.
- **When required by law**, if we receive a valid legal request (limited to information we actually hold, which is typically none beyond what third parties process).

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

- Local data (blocked nicks, preferences, cache) remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the App.
- IRC networks retain data according to their own policies; contact the relevant network operator to exercise rights there.
- To limit ad-related data collection, you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings or opt out of personalized ads where your device OS allows.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will assist with removal of locally stored App data.

## 8. Security

IRC is a decades-old protocol. Unless you connect to a network that supports TLS and you configure a secure connection, messages may be readable in transit. Use strong, unique NickServ passwords and follow each IRC network's security guidance.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
